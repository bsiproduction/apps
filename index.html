ShopCall · Web app · shop @30 · Android 1.4

The shop, off paper.

ShopCall is the everyday way the crew works with the shop's Google Sheets. Orders, pulls, intakes, loans, recounts and the board, on a phone in the truck bay or on the desk in the office.

  1. Today

    Opens on what needs you.

    Each role gets its own Today: what to act on, what's coming, and whether anything is broken. When the list is empty it says so. When it couldn't check, it says that instead of pretending all is clear.

  2. Orders

    Start an order from the calendar.

    A gig on the calendar with “No order yet” gets a Start its order button. ShopCall clones the Shop Order Template and registers the gig as a DRAFT.

  3. Order Builder

    Type it the way you'd say it.

    One search box: 6 sm58. Free counts are live for those dates, with other orders and loans already subtracted. Bundles, companions, a “usually goes along” list, and shortage fixes with a sub-rent or a stand-in. Then print case labels and Release to the shop.

  4. Shop Board

    The bulletin board, the shopping list, done today.

    Post to the board from any phone. Shopping-list items move from open to ordered to received. Inspections and cleaning show up under Done today.

Pulls, line by line

The phone pull screen walks the order. Ring means not pulled, dot means pulled, truck means on the truck. If the order changes mid-pull, the line says so and asks for a Got it.

Intakes when the gear comes back

Count lines back in, report damage, add a note. Short or missing lines can go straight to the shopping list.

Three roles

Shop techs on the floor, the office with Today, Gigs, Board and Tasks, and the desk with orders, approvals, errors and config.

Notes and feedback

Anyone can leave a Note. It stays on each person's Today until they tap Seen. Send feedback goes to a shared inbox at the desk.

6 am digest

A morning email with the same checks as the app's needs-you list, plus a post-gig report and Google Chat events.

Truck plan on the wall

Loader's pack shows up in ShopCall to scale, nose to doors, curb side and street side, and on a wall display that refreshes itself.

On every phone

One crew link, bsiproduction.github.io. A BSI tile on the iPhone home screen, an Android app with no address bar, and a built-in QR scanner that opens an item or a case from its tag.

Each page wears its stone

Today is sandstone, Gigs are lapis, Inventory is jade, Approvals are amethyst. The Mineral header palette tells you where you are before you read a word.

A gig, from calendar to truck

What actually happens in the app, in order.

  1. No order yetThe gig lands on the calendar. Today offers Start its order.
  2. Build itSearch, bundles, companions, fix the shortages, one gig per case.
  3. ReleasePrint case labels. The tech's Today now shows Start pull.

StandBy · Mac · 0.2.15 · Companion module 1.0.4

One Mac runs the show. Every GO on the Space bar.

The caller holds the run of show. Every other laptop, tablet and phone on the Wi-Fi follows along in a browser, with nothing to install. A second Mac runs the teleprompter.

  1. Caller

    Standby, Live, GO.

    Three cards across the top with time left and whether the show is running early or late. Space is GO, ⌘Z backs one up, S sets Standby, ⌘B is the big clock. Below it, the whole run of show by department.

  2. Display

    A wall screen that says it plainly.

    /display shows Show on time, what's Live with its clock, and what's on Standby with everything that goes with it. Arrange it by name from any laptop: ?name=Green room.

  3. Timer

    A speaker clock the size of the wall.

    /timer is a countdown for the person on stage. /script scrolls the prompter script in step with the show.

  4. Prompter

    Paste the script. Mark the cues.

    Paste from a Google Doc or Word. A ⟦12⟧ mark shows where caller cue 12 falls, so the caller sees “N lines to go”. Mirror for beam-splitter glass, 4:3, 16:10 or 16:9, and a speaker timer you can drag to size.

  5. Talent screen

    What the speaker sees.

    Big, clean, and locked down in Show mode so nothing can disturb them. Practice mode rehearses privately while the crew screens stop following. Pace replays the rehearsal's reading speed.

Follow on any phone

Scan the QR on the caller screen, pick your department, and see your Standby, what's Live and what's coming up. Editors get /edit with a PIN and can fix cues but never GO.

From the Google Sheet

Write the run of show in the BSI template. Check ROS, Send to Host, and Pull Show Results afterwards. CSV is the fallback with no internet.

Messages and banners

M sends a silent text to the crew screens, and they can answer. Green banners close themselves after 8 seconds. Red ones wait for OK.

Rehearsal mode

Before the first GO, times are recorded without counting as the real show. Rehearsal is violet, Standby is amber, Live is green, late is red.

Stream Deck

The Bitfocus Companion module has a 15-key starter deck coloured like the caller screen. End Show needs a 2-second hold. A key can GO but never change the ROS.

Finds the caller on its own

The Prompter lists “StandBy on this network” over Bonjour. A caller it can see but can't reach shows greyed out with what to check.

Never updates during a show

Check for Updates… pulls small signed packages from BSI's own feed. It waits until the show is over, and Go Back to Previous Version… undoes it.

Show day

From the Sheet to the last GO.

  1. Send to HostIn the Sheet's StandBy menu: Check ROS, then Send to Host.
  2. Load the showOpen StandBy, pick Caller, ⌘O the load.json.
  3. Crew followThey scan the QR and pick a department. Wall screens open /display.
  4. Prompter linksThe second Mac picks Prompter, enters the PIN and pastes the script.
  5. Call itSpace for every GO. The clock tracks early or late.
  6. End ShowClocks stop, results save, and the Sheet pulls them back.

PatchMap · Mac · 1.15.0

Draw the rig once.

PatchMap turns one schematic into the patch sheet, the cable labels, the packlist and the shop order. Audio, video, lighting, network and power, in racks, on tables and in zones.

  1. Schematic ⌘1

    Drop gear. Click port to port.

    Pull devices from the Library into Racks, Tables and Zones, or onto a venue map. Click a port, then another, to run a cable. Zoomed out, each device shows its name and how many ports are used.

  2. Inspector

    Every port, every label.

    Zoom in and the ports come back. A connector mismatch warns you, with Patch anyway for adapters. Cable ID, label and length live in the Inspector.

  3. Racks

    Open a rack, see what's inside.

    A dashed ring on a rack port means it's patched inside. Built racks save to the library and come back whole next time.

  4. Patch sheet ⌘2

    One line per cable.

    From, to, connector and label, with a tick box for patching on site. Assign cable IDs…, Print labels, Copy as text, or Print / PDF the whole pack.

  5. Packlist ⌘3

    Everything to pack, with Out and Back.

    Grouped by rack and zone. Live stock comes from ShopCall, and a red “N short” pill shows what the shop can't cover before you send the order.

  2. Lay it outAdd Racks and Zones, click Library items in, drag them onto racks.

Loader · Mac · 0.4.0

Plan the truck before anyone lifts a case.

Loader packs a gig's cases into a truck in 3D, with BSI's own case library and the rules that go with it. Then it prints the labels, the checklist and the unloading order.

  1. The load

    A 26' box, packed nose to doors.

    Drag a case in, or press + to put it in the next open spot. R turns it, T tips it, N pushes it to the nose. The list on the right is the loading order the crew reads from.

  2. Colour by what's inside

    See the speakers from across the shop.

    Every physical case is linked to its contents in the Pack Repo. Switch on Colour by What's Inside and the truck lights up by group: speakers, amp racks, drive, splits, consoles.

  3. From above

    Four views, one plan.

    3D, Above, Side and Doors. The PDF prints all four, with a loading step by step and an Unloading at the venue page, last in first out, grouped by PatchMap zones.

  4. Open Shop Order

    The order becomes the To pack list.

    File › Open Shop Order… reads the gig through BSI Link. Pack cases one at a time with Enter, or Pack the rest and flip through the best three plans.

  5. Checklist

    In and Out, on paper or on a phone.

    The loadout checklist lists every case with an In box and an Out box. Send Pack Numbers writes each case's truck, position and step back to the order, so ShopCall can draw it.

Case types, 77 pieces

The 200, 300 and 350 series, rack cases and HDL and GTS karts, from the shop walkthrough, with size, weight and how many BSI owns.

Rules the shop already has

This side up, nothing on top, fragile, on wheels, wheel cups, stays on the floor. A 300 tips onto its end, never its side.

Blocks the bad stack

No overlaps, nothing through a wall, nothing on a no-stack case. Wheeled cases only sit in matching wheel cups. Warns on overhang and heavy over light.

Which truck do I need?

Sprinter, 16', 24' and 26' box, 48' and 53' trailers, Isuzu and Penske presets with E-track rows. Choose a truck trial-packs every one and picks the smallest that holds everything.

Import from a walkthrough

Paste the transcript: “Amp rack A, 30 by 24 by 40, 220 pounds, this side up.” Loader reads it into the case library, or takes a CSV.

Load bars and straps

Suggest Load Bars and Straps places them on the E-track rows. B and S add them by hand.

Pack labels

Avery 5164, striped in the gig's tape colour, with a QR that opens the case in ShopCall.

TrussTape · Mac · 0.1.1 Beta

Stick the plot on the truss.

TrussTape takes a Vectorworks Spotlight plot and prints full-scale hang tapes on an ordinary 80 mm receipt printer. Every fixture's label sits exactly where that fixture hangs.

  1. Positions

    Every truss and boom, with its fixtures.

    Run the export script in Vectorworks and TrussTape opens the plot. Pick Truss 1, tick the label lines you want, and the pages preview as they'll print, so no label ever straddles a join.

  2. The tape

    Unit, channel, address, colour, purpose.

    Each label prints on a ruler in feet and inches or metres. Zero at the centre of the position or the start of the tape. Flip it to read from upstage. Cut at the ✂ lines and butt the pages.

AS HUNGSample Corporate Ballroom, 24 fixtures, 3 positions

From Vectorworks, without touching the drawing

The export script writes every Lighting Device and Light Position to a .tape.json and opens TrussTape. Or drop in the CSV from Export Instrument Data.

1:1

Or a pocket strip

1:2 to 1:12 for a reference you can carry, from the same plot.

Any roll

80 mm receipt, 58 mm, Dymo 2¼" continuous, or custom. Page length and paper loss are adjustable.

Feed calibration

Print a 500 mm strip, measure it, type the number. A percent is inches over a 40' truss, so the tape is stretched to match.

Print or PDF

Print this position, Print all, Save PDF, Save all as PDF. Any CUPS printer with a custom roll size.

First build

0.1.1 is the first test build. It prints to PDF today and is waiting on its first run on a thermal printer and its first run of the script inside Vectorworks. Feedback from that test shapes 0.2.

Plot to truss

The whole thing, from the drawing to the hang.

  1. ExportTools › Scripts › Run Script… › TrussTape Export.py.
  2. Pick the positionTruss 1, and the label lines you want on it.
  3. 80 mm at 1:1Print this position.
  4. Cut and buttTear at the ✂ lines and join the pages end to end.
  5. Stick it onHang each fixture at its label.

Under all of them

One kit. Four Mac apps. The same hands.

StandBy, PatchMap, Loader and TrussTape share an updater, a feedback window, a manual and a design system, so learning one means you already know the others.

Signed self-updates

Check for Updates… pulls a small package from BSI's public feed. Each release is signed with a key that exists only on the release Mac. An app refuses anything whose signature, size or hash doesn't match. Go Back to Previous Version… is one click.

A manual in every app

Help › Manual, ⇧⌘/, with search, What's New and guided tours on the ? key.

Send Feedback…

One shared collector. It lands in the desk's inbox in ShopCall.

One look

Montserrat for words, IBM Plex Mono for numbers, light and dark, and one accent per app.

Apple Silicon

macOS 12 or later. Installed once from a Drive zip, then it updates itself.
